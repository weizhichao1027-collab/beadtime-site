How to make a board

Turn a photo into a pattern

On Home, start a photo-to-pattern project and pick an image from Photos, the camera, or Files. Set crop, board size, color limit, detail mode, and reference palette. A careful default is 40×40 with 16 colors. Leave smart background removal off unless the subject is clear and the background gets in the way. Watch the live preview, generate, then confirm against the original or go back and retune.

Count materials and shop

Open Materials to see bead counts by color. Tick colors you already have, estimate extra packs, and copy the shopping list. On-screen codes are approximate. Check the brand's current physical chart before a large purchase.

Follow along by color

In Follow, pick one color so the board highlights only that color. Tap placed beads to record progress, watch overall completion, and see the next color. Keep the screen awake if you need it. Progress stays in the local project.

Export and back up

Export PNG or PDF with coordinates from the workspace or project menu. Large boards such as 58×58 include 20×20 section pages. Before you change phones or delete the app, export a JSON backup from Me → Settings and Privacy. Uninstalling deletes every pattern in the sandbox.

Common questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos you pick with the system picker, camera, or Files are processed only on the device. Smart background removal uses Apple Vision on the device. Photos and masks are not sent to a server.

Why does the pattern look off?

Results depend on source sharpness, crop, board size, color limit, and detail mode. The usual quality path is 40×40 with 16 colors. Use the live preview, confirm against the original, or go back and retune. You can also regenerate from the original after saving.

How does smart background removal work?

It is off by default. When enabled, it protects the subject on the device and treats connected background as empty cells. If edges vanish or background remains, turn it off and edit empty cells by hand, or use a clearer subject photo.

Can I buy beads from the on-screen codes?

Treat codes as approximate screen references, not official factory matches. Displays and material batches vary. Compare the brand's current physical chart before a large purchase.

Where do PNG and PDF exports go?

Export from the workspace or project menu. PNG can be saved to Photos with add-only access; the app does not read your library with that permission. PDFs include coordinates. Large boards such as 58×58 add 20×20 section pages. Other sharing uses the system share sheet.

A project is missing or will not open.

Check library search filters and sorting first, then clear the search. Keep the app installed and preserve its data. If Settings still opens, export a backup before contacting support with diagnostics. Invalid projects are isolated locally so readable projects can remain available.

How do backups merge?

Export a JSON backup from Me → Settings and Privacy, then import it on the other device. Projects merge by ID, keeping the most recently updated copy. The current backup schema is 1. Unsupported schemas are rejected. If a format error appears, update the app and keep both the original file and a known working backup.

Does uninstalling keep my patterns?

No. Uninstalling deletes sandbox patterns, previews, and progress. Export a local backup before you remove the app or change phones.

How do I copy diagnostics?

Go to Me → Settings and Privacy → Copy Diagnostic Information. It includes app version, device, system, and local project count. It does not include photos or patterns. Paste it into your support email.

Which languages are included?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Arabic (right to left), and Hindi. Follow the system or lock a language in Settings.

Why does the app ask for the camera?

Only when you choose to take a photo for a pattern. That photo is also processed on the device. The app does not use location, microphone, contacts, or tracking.

Does it work without a network?

Yes. Core making is fully offline. You only need a network to open this site or send email. The app does not contact a developer server.

Is the app free?

Yes. Fuse Beads is free on the App Store. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or accounts. Open it and start a board.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Layouts support portrait, landscape, smaller windows, and larger text. Check the App Store compatibility list for other devices.

What if camera or photo saving access is denied?

Find Fuse Beads in system Settings, allow Camera or add-only Photos access as needed, then retry. Photo import uses the system picker for only the photos you select; Files is another option. Keep the app installed while resolving permissions.

How do iPad layouts and text size work?

Rotate the device or resize its multitasking window; the layout adapts to available width. Change text size in system Settings. The app supports Dynamic Type, including accessibility sizes, and equivalent VoiceOver controls.

What if a backup fails or is too large?

Check available device storage and whether the destination is writable, keep your previous backup, and retry. A backup is limited to 40 MiB, with at most 200 projects after merging. If it is too large, export PNG/PDF copies of important projects and contact support before deleting projects or uninstalling. PNG/PDF files do not replace an editable JSON backup.
